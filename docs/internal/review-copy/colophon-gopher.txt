This is a Gopher page
=====================

You are reading this at gopher://localhost:7070/ — served to you by the Unseen Servant (usv) Gopher server.

Unseen Servant, and why "usv"
-----------------------------

Unseen Servant is a small server that publishes one folder of writing to several internet protocols at once. The short name takes the letters from the long one:

UnSeen serVant  ->  u  s  v

It is abbreviated rather than spelled out because it is also a command you type.

About Gopher
------------

Gopher has been serving menus since 1991 and has not changed its mind since. It treats the internet as a filing cabinet you browse rather than a library of documents you read. That refusal to evolve is the point: software written in the nineties still works, and what you publish today will very likely still be readable by clients nobody has touched in decades.

  Gopherspace, over the web — https://gopher.floodgap.com/
  RFC 1436 — the Gopher specification (1993) — https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc1436
